# v0.2 Design

v0.2 of the planar ring antenna is designed according to the specifications of [Rogers Corporation RT/Duroid 6010.2 Laminate](Literature/RT-duroid_6006-6010LM_Laminate_Data_Sheet.pdf). Unlike v0.1, it does not include a solder mask on top of the antenna, allowing the diamond to sit closer to the resonant magnetic fields, but potentially lowering durability. All simulations and models were created in CST Studio.
