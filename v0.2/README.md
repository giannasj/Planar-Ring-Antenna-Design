## v0.2 Design
# Overview
v0.2 of the planar ring antenna is designed according to the specifications of Rogers Corporation RT/Duroid 6010.2 Laminate. Unlike v0.1, it does not include a solder mask on top of the antenna, allowing the diamond to sit closer to the resonant magnetic fields, but potentially lowering durability.
